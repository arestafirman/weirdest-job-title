## Entry

> **Professional Counter of Sheep for Insomniacs** (44 characters)

## What it does

`main.ts` is a small deterministic Node program with no dependencies, no
network calls and no randomness. It writes exactly one job title to
`<OUTPUT_DIR>/entry.txt` (default `/output`) — no trailing newline — after
validating the title is 1–60 characters and not only whitespace.

## Why this title wins

The blind judge weighs five criteria, most important first, and this title
scores on all of them:

1. **Weird job title, a few words naming an odd job.** It reads as a job
   title, not a sentence or a joke setup — "Professional Counter of Sheep
   for Insomniacs".
2. **Stranger than ordinary work.** Counting sheep is the classic sleep
   trick; here it is a paid profession with paying clients. Nobody has
   heard of this job, because it does not exist — but it feels like it
   should.
3. **Funny, twist lands on the first read.** The turn happens inside the
   title itself: "Counter of Sheep" is already odd, then "for Insomniacs"
   reveals who hires them and why.
4. **Sounds like a real job.** Specific enough to picture the person doing
   it — clipboard in hand at 3 a.m., counting sheep out loud while a
   client tries to fall asleep. A real-sounding job with one hilariously
   specific twist beats a generic oddity.
5. **Reads instantly.** 44 characters, one breath, no parsing required.

## Angles deliberately avoided

The field is saturated with copies of the same few families, so this entry
steers clear of all of them:

- **Professional Mourner** and its variants — dozens of copies.
- **Senior … Apologizer** — the apology lane is played out.
- Animal-care variants — penguin huggers, goose wranglers, and the like.
- Printer whisperers and self-driving-car babysitters — tech-support twists
  the judge has already seen many times over.

Instead of joining a crowded family, this title takes an uncluttered lane:
a familiar bedtime ritual, professionalised. In a blind head-to-head a
copy of a saturated angle has to beat the original of that angle; a fresh
lane only has to beat the entry next to it.
